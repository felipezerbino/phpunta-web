Punta del Este Guide

Punta del Este, told by the people who live it.

Everything worth knowing before you come: which area to stay in, when the season is, how to get here and how a stay with us works.

Updated on November 17, 2026ES · PT · EN
In short

Punta del Este is Uruguay’s best-known seaside resort, in the department of Maldonado, about 135 km (84 miles) from Montevideo. It is built around the Peninsula, with two very different beaches on each side (Mansa, with calm water, and Brava, with waves), and stretches east through La Barra, Manantiales and José Ignacio, and west through Pinares and Punta Ballena. High season runs from December 15 to the end of February.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Punta del Este?

It depends on the trip. To walk everywhere and be close to restaurants, the Peninsula. With kids and to swim without waves, Playa Mansa. For open ocean and full-service buildings, Playa Brava. For dining and nightlife, La Barra. For calm and privacy, José Ignacio, Punta Ballena or the country estates of Laguna del Sauce.

When is high season in Punta del Este?

From December 15 to the end of February, peaking between Christmas and mid-January. Carnival and Tourism Week (Holy Week) are also in high demand.

How far is the Punta del Este airport?

Punta del Este airport is in Laguna del Sauce, about 20 km (12 miles) from the Peninsula: around 25 minutes by car.

How long does it take from Montevideo to Punta del Este?

About 135 km (84 miles) by road: around 2 hours by car to the Peninsula.

How far is José Ignacio from Punta del Este?

About 38 km (24 miles) from the Peninsula, around 40 minutes by car, through La Barra and Manantiales.
