Using the website, sending inquiries, requesting availability and/or making a booking imply acceptance of these Terms of Service, together with the Privacy Policy, the Cookie Policy and the specific conditions reported for each property.

3. Booking process

A booking shall only be considered finalized once the following have occurred, as applicable:

a. express confirmation of availability;

b. proper identification of the lead guest;

c. acceptance of applicable terms;

d. full or partial payment as required; and

e. issuance of confirmation by PHPunta or the enabled booking system.

PHPunta may reject booking requests when there are inconsistencies in the data provided, operational impossibility, prior breaches by the guest, specific property restrictions, or reasonable reasons of security, coexistence or regulatory compliance.

4. Prices, currency and taxes

Published prices may be expressed in US dollars, Uruguayan pesos or another currency indicated on the site. Unless otherwise stated, amounts correspond to the value of the stay and may not include additional charges such as processing fees, cleaning, security deposits, fees, taxes, extraordinary consumption or other items duly reported before final confirmation.

PHPunta strives to keep rate information up to date; however, until the booking is confirmed, prices and conditions may be modified without prior notice.

Payments may be made through electronic gateways, cards, bank transfers or other means enabled by PHPunta.

The guest warrants that they are using their own or duly authorized payment methods. Every transaction will be subject to security validations, fund confirmation, anti-fraud controls and/or additional verifications that PHPunta, the payment gateway or the card issuer deem necessary.

6. Security deposit and additional authorizations

For certain properties or bookings, PHPunta may require a security deposit, pre-authorization, temporary hold, documentary guarantee or equivalent mechanism to cover damages, missing items, extraordinary consumption, breaches, fines or costs attributable to the guest or their companions.

The conditions of such guarantee —amount, method, release deadline and total or partial retention scenarios— will be reported before or during the booking process.

7. Check-in, check-out and identification

Entry and exit times will be those reported for each booking or property. Compliance is essential for operational coordination.

The lead guest must provide truthful information and, when requested, verify their identity, list of occupants, contact data and other elements reasonably necessary for managing the stay.

PHPunta may deny access or cancel the booking if:

a. the guest's identity does not match the booking;

b. undeclared occupants are detected;

c. the maximum authorized capacity is exceeded;

e. documentary or payment requirements are not met.

8. Permitted use of the property

The guest undertakes to use the property and its facilities in a diligent and responsible manner, consistent with its intended use, respecting at all times:

a. the maximum authorized capacity;

b. coexistence and good behavior rules;

c. the internal regulations of the building, neighborhood or complex;

e. the operational instructions communicated by PHPunta.

Unless expressly authorized in writing, the following are prohibited:

  • holding parties, events or mass gatherings;

Non-compliance may result in immediate cancellation of the stay, retention of amounts, loss of the security deposit and damage claims.
