Cookie policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies are data storage and retrieval files or devices that are downloaded to the user's device when they access certain websites. They allow remembering information about the visit, recognizing the user, improving their experience, analyzing browsing habits and optimizing site operation.

2. What types of cookies this site uses

This site may use first-party and third-party cookies, including:

a. Technical or necessary cookies

These are essential for the basic functioning of the site, navigation, security, session management, access to specific areas and use of the booking engine.

b. Preference or personalization cookies

They allow remembering settings such as language, device type, browsing preferences or other parameters that improve the user experience.

c. Analytics or measurement cookies

They allow quantifying visits, measuring site interaction, understanding usage patterns and improving content, usability and overall site performance.

d. Advertising or marketing cookies

They may be used to display more relevant content or ads, measure campaigns, create audiences, perform remarketing or understand the origin of commercial conversions.

3. Purpose of cookie use

Cookies used on this site may be employed, among other purposes, to:

  • enable proper browsing and site operation;
  • facilitate the search and booking process;
  • remember user preferences;
  • analyze traffic and browsing behavior;
  • measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns;
  • improve content, services and site performance;
  • strengthen security measures and fraud prevention.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be installed by third parties providing technology services linked to the site or booking engine, such as analytical tools, payment gateways, advertising integrations, booking platforms, players, maps, forms or support services.

The use of these cookies by third parties is also governed by their own policies.

5. Cookie management

The user may configure their browser to accept, block, limit or delete cookies. Disabling certain cookies may affect the normal operation of the site or partially or totally prevent some services, including the booking process.

6. Consent

When applicable, browsing this site and accepting the cookie configuration will imply the user's consent to their use under the terms described here.

7. Modifications

PHPunta may update this Cookie Policy when necessary due to legal, regulatory, technical or operational changes. It is recommended to review this section periodically.
